📋 How to Install in Gmail in 20 Seconds (خطوات التفعيل في جيميل)
1

Click the big green button "Copy Rich Signature" above to copy the formatted signature directly to your clipboard.

2

Open Gmail, click the Settings Cog ⚙️ in the top right → See all settings → scroll down to Signature.

3

Create a new signature, click into the signature box, press Ctrl + V (Paste), then scroll to the bottom and click Save Changes!

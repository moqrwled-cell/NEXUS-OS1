Welcome to the Nexus Lead Extractor module. This system will allow you to autonomously scrape thousands of high-value B2B leads (Emails, Phone Numbers, Websites) directly from global directories in minutes.

🛠️ System Requirements

To initialize this node, you need zero coding experience. You will use our trusted third-party scraping engine.

  • Engine: Apify (Google Maps Scraper)
  • Cost: $5 free monthly credit (enough for 5,000+ leads).

🚀 Deployment Steps

Step 1: Initialize the Engine

  1. Go to Apify.com and create a free account.
  2. Navigate to the Store and search for Google Maps Scraper.
  3. Click Try for free to add the scraper to your console.

Step 2: Configure the Target Parameters

Do not use the default settings. Copy and paste the exact Nexus configurations below to ensure high-quality lead generation.

  • Search Terms: Enter your highly specific niche (e.g., Dental Clinics in Dubai, Real Estate Agencies in London).
  • Location: Specify the exact city or coordinates.
  • Max Places Per Search: 500
  • Extract Emails/Contacts: ✅ True (Crucial for outreach).

Step 3: Execute and Extract

  1. Click the green Start button at the bottom of the page.
  2. The system will now autonomously scrape the web. Wait 2-5 minutes.
  3. Once completed, navigate to the Export tab.
  4. Select CSV or Excel format.
Pro Tip: Clean your downloaded Excel sheet by sorting the "Email" column. Delete any rows that do not contain an email address to ensure your outreach campaigns have a 100% deliverability rate.
